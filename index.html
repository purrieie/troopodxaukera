02  ·  Problems

Four problems found on the live Aukera site

Found by auditing Aukera's live pages, the Meta Ad Library and the Raíz Solitaire Diamond Ring PDP. Each maps to a specific fix in the solutions section that follows.

PROBLEM 01  ·  CAMPAIGN LANDING PAGES
Solved by TrooLaunch →

Aukera already builds campaign pages. They read like a form, not a collection

Roughly 450 ads are live on Meta, led by bridal rings with a hidden blue diamond, the Wildflower collection, Hearts and Arrows solitaires and a Karwa Chauth mangalsutra push. Aukera gives several of these their own page, which is the right instinct and more than most brands at this scale do.

The pages follow one template: a hero, an enquiry form, then a grid of pieces. The things that sell a ₹1 lakh ring, the story, the craft, the ring on a hand, the full look, sit at the bottom or are missing.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  BRIDAL ADS

The best story Aukera has is the last thing on the page

On the bridal limited drop page, the hidden blue diamond, a one-cent stone set inside every bridal ring for luck, is explained in a paragraph below 12 rings with More Details buttons and no prices. On an editorial page it would be the opening chapter.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  ADS

An ad running in 11 versions answers a question the site never does

Aukera's 'no dosham' ad, live since 1 October, reassures buyers that its diamonds carry no dosham. No page, collection or product description on the site mentions dosham, so a buyer who clicks for that answer does not find it.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  TIMING

Dhanteras is a month away and has no page yet

Dhanteras is 6 November, and the only Diwali collection on the site is the Valentine's range under a new name. The Karwa Chauth page also never tells buyers to order by 14 October to beat the 7 to 10 day dispatch.

PROBLEM 02  ·  SITE PERFORMANCE AND VISIBILITY
Solved by TrooPerformance →

The site is slow on mobile, and the same weight blocks search and AI

Google PageSpeed Insights, Lighthouse, mobile, was run against the home page, the pendants collection and the Hogar Solitaire product page. All three score between 30 and 42. Real user data over the last 28 days fails Core Web Vitals on the home page and the product page, so this is not a lab artifact.

On the home page, two autoplay videos alone weigh 20.3 MB of a 26.7 MB page.

Google's threshold for a good Largest Contentful Paint is under 2.5 seconds. The home page is 9x past it and the pendants page 8x. Blocking Time should be under 200ms, and the product page is at 2,417ms, 12x past it.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  SEARCH AND AI VISIBILITY

The same markup problems make Aukera hard to read for Google and for AI agents

Lighthouse now scores Agentic Browsing, how usable a site is to AI shopping agents. Aukera scores 5 out of 6 on all three pages, and the gaps overlap with the ones that hurt search.

  • The markup is not well formed. The accessibility tree fails on all three pages, which is the one Agentic Browsing check Aukera misses.
  • Most images are unlabelled. 128 of 174 images on the home page, 133 of 190 on the pendants page and 274 of 384 on the Raíz product page have no alt text.
  • The proof answer engines quote is missing. The product schema carries no rating or review, and /pages/faq is live with a single word on it, the heading.

PROBLEM 03  ·  SEEING THE RING ONLINE
Solved by Troo3D →

A buyer cannot judge a ₹1 lakh ring from three photos

In a store, a buyer turns the ring under the light, slips it on and asks the consultant about every stone. Online, the Raíz page gives her three packshots on a grey background. No hand shot, no video, and no way to see how 1.44 carats sits on a finger or how the side stones catch the light.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  LIVE ON SITE

Aukera already shoots rings on hands, just not for the product page

The homepage category tiles show bridal rings worn on a hand. The Raíz product page carries three studio images and no video, and so does the Hogar Solitaire. The one thing the store does best, letting her see the ring properly, has no equivalent online.

PROBLEM 04  ·  CONVERSION BARRIERS
Solved by TrooCRO →

Specific barriers on the Raíz Solitaire PDP are costing the sale

We read the Raíz Solitaire Diamond Ring page the way a 28 year old shopping for her own engagement ring would, on her phone, and listed every point where she would feel unsure, confused or let down. That surfaced eleven barriers. The six most likely to make her close the tab are below. Prices as of 7 October 2026.

TRUST

Aukera has far more proof than this page shows

Aukera has 35 stores, IGI grading on every diamond, an 80% diamond buy-back on pieces under ₹3 lakh, and Taapsee Pannu fronting its live solitaire ads. On this page she sees an IGI logo, a Lifetime Exchange icon and not a single review.

CONFUSING

She picks her size and the price jumps by ₹9,500

The ring opens at ₹110,126 in size 10. Size 12 drops it to ₹100,604, size 13 takes it to ₹112,020, and nothing says why. The price itself holds, the same on the page, in the cart and at checkout, but her first thought is that the cheaper size has a smaller diamond.

VALUE

She can't tell if ₹1.1 lakh is a fair price

The page shows 2.815 grams of 18KT gold, 1.44 carats of diamonds and one total. No split between gold, diamonds, making charges and GST, so she cannot see what the diamonds cost or hold this up against another jeweller's quote.

AFFORDABILITY

₹1.1 lakh, and no monthly option in sight

EMI is not mentioned anywhere on the page. Aukera's own monthly purchase plan exists, but it is redeemable only in stores and is not linked from here.

FIT

She doesn't know her ring size, and a wrong guess can't be returned

View Size Chart opens a table of numbers from 8 to 21 with no way to measure at home, and only sizes 10, 11, 12, 13 and 16 are on offer. Change of mind returns are not accepted, and the page never says whether a wrong size can be swapped.

VISIT

Try at home breaks the purchase midway and sends her to WhatsApp

She is one step from deciding, taps At-Home Trial or Reserve in Store, and is pushed off the site into a WhatsApp chat to wait for a consultant. Her chosen size does not go with her, and nothing tells her whether the trial is free, whether it reaches her city, or which of Aukera's 35 stores has this ring.

See the remaining barriers found on this PDP
MISSING

The ad promised a hidden blue diamond. This ring's page never mentions one

Aukera's bridal ads and its bridal page say every Aukera bridal ring hides a one-cent blue diamond for luck. The Raíz is tagged Bridal and Engagement and sits in Bridal Picks, yet its page has no line, photo or detail about it. The most personal part of an engagement ring is missing from the page where she decides.

CLARITY

The spec panel speaks jeweller, not customer

VVS2, VVS, E and 1.44 Carats sit there with no explanation of what they mean or whether they are good. And 1.44 carats is the whole ring, centre stone, side stones and band together, not the centre stone size she would tell her friends about. There is no IGI report number either.

DOUBT

Nothing on the page answers "is this a real diamond?"

Lab grown is mentioned once, in the description. Aukera has pages on lab grown versus mined diamonds and how to choose lab grown, and none is linked here. The Design, Innovation and Craftsmanship tabs she opens for answers talk about the company, word for word the same as on the Hogar ring.

RISK

Shipping, returns and delivery are left to guesswork

Shipping is free across India, yet the page says it is calculated at checkout, and she only sees FREE after typing her full address. Change of mind returns are not accepted and dispatch takes 7 to 10 business days. Neither the page nor the checkout shows a delivery date.

POLISH

Buttons sit on top of other buttons

On the first photo the wishlist heart is drawn over the zoom icon, and next to Add to Cart the WhatsApp bubble covers the wishlist button. Small, but on a lakh rupee ring, small glitches read as careless.
